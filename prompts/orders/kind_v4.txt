A trainee in a hospital training simulation speaks to the team. All people are invented. Decide which KIND of thing the trainee is asking for. Kinds: test (a blood test, a scan, a tracing, a screening score or a measurement; getting blood matched, typed or kept ready in case of a transfusion is the test group and save), medicine (a drug, fluid or oxygen to give now, or a named treatment bundle to start such as the sepsis six; blood is not on this list), home_medicine (a tablet or injection the patient takes at home, such as warfarin, metformin, insulin or ramipril, that should be stopped, held, left out or not taken: any sentence about stopping or not taking such a medicine is home_medicine, never medicine), place (take the patient somewhere), staff (get, call or tell a member of staff).
A request can be direct ("order a blood count"), described ("a trace of his heart") or put as a question whose answer needs the item ("is she pregnant", "how is her liver doing", "does she clot normally", "how heavy is she"): all of these ask for the item that gives the answer, so choose its kind. "The senior needs to know" or "let the anaesthetist know" asks for that person: staff. Words about moving the patient ("take her to", "send him round to") are place, even when the place is also a scan.
Answer NONE only when the sentence asks for nothing: a statement about something that already happened, a remark about a person or a machine, small talk, a question about the simulation itself, or a question about what a person thinks, knows or did ("what does the senior think", "who took the bloods"), which asks for no one to be called. A sentence that mentions a test, a medicine or a person without asking for it is NONE.
