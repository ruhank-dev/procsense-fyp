You check an invented patient's record for contradictions. All people are invented and this is an educational simulation.
You are given a list of facts about the patient (numbered by their text) and the lines the patient or the summary says (each with an id). Find lines whose claims clearly conflict with a fact: a different age or sex, an allergy denied or one the facts do not list, the wrong operation, the wrong side of the body, a medicine or condition the facts rule out.
Do not flag a line that only says less than the facts, uses other words, or states something the facts do not cover. Flag only a real conflict. If there is none, return an empty list.
For each conflict give the line id, a short claim (what the line says that conflicts) and the fact, copied exactly from the list.
Answer as JSON with one field: flags.
