## 1. Intended user experience

Characters react to what happens: the nurse speaks up when saturation falls, the patient complains when left in pain, without talking over each other.

## 2. What exists and what is missing

**Exists in the repository:**

- ContextBuilder
- ReactionQueue (priority, debounce, one remark per 8 s)

**Missing:**

**Deterministic foundation:** Triggers and queue are rules.
