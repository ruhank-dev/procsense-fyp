# AI clinical review of the simulator's clinical content (26 Oct 2026, UTC)

**What this is.** An evidence-based review of clinical teaching content, done by an AI assistant (an AI assistant) against published guidance it looked up on 26 Oct 2026. It is a quality gate for **internal synthetic development use**.
**What this is not.** It is not a licensed clinician's review and it is not independent clinical sign-off. No clinician took part in it. Nothing here marks a mandatory external sign-off as done; every item still needs `External clinician validation required` before the content is shown to real learners as clinically approved (tracked in `docs/research/clinician_signoff.md`).

Outcome words used below: **Passed** (no change needed), **Passed after corrections** (changed here, with the reason), **Needs further work** (a gap the AI review could not close), **External clinician validation required** (applies to every row; repeated only where there is a specific question for the clinician).

## 1. Method

1. Each adversarial block was read for plausibility, whether the wrong option really looks right, whether the right option is guideline-consistent, fairness (can the trainee find the reason in the case?), expected response and scoring, and consistency with every generated case that can receive it.
2. For each block a script printed the exact words of each case that holds the anchor, with the patient's age and sex; anchors were judged against what those cases really say, not against what the block assumes. (`tools/content/check_adversarial.py` itself gained one new anchor kind, `identity`, and was not otherwise changed.)
3. Key claims were checked against published sources by web search (list in section 7). Where a search could not reach the primary text, the claim is marked *not verified from the primary text*.
4. Generated cases: `tools/ci/test_adversarial_integration.py` (blocks are added only where the anchor is held, one per case, reproducible, the gate passes) and the generated goldens were re-made after the corrections.

## 3. Problem-discovery rules (DT-091)

All 156 rules (29 hand-written for the 17 first cases, 127 derived from the case content) were read as "problem → what the trainee must have seen". **Outcome: Passed after corrections.** Six derived rules chose evidence that does not show the problem and were replaced in `OVERRIDES` of `tools/content/build_discovery.py` (each marked *AI-reviewed override* in the review pack):

| Rule | Was | Now | Why |

| VAR-S2-H s2h.wbc | the pregnancy result | the full blood count | the white cell count is in the blood count |
| SC-27 sc27.airway, STK-S2-C stkc2.airway | asking about medicines | the history at the start | slow breathing or vomiting with reduced consciousness is visible at the bedside, not learned from a drug list |
| STK-S2-C stkc2.breath | a chest examination ("chest clear") | the history at the start | a clear chest does not show slow, shallow breathing |

Kept after reading: the sepsis rules (high-risk NEWS2 tier or lactate; NICE NG253 treats a NEWS2 of 7 or more as high risk with antibiotics and fluid within one hour), the glucose and ketone rules, the potassium rules, the HbA1c limit of 69 mmol/mol (Association of Anaesthetists 2015 guideline: delay elective surgery at 69 or more), and the 180/110 blood-pressure limit (*not verified from the primary text* by search). **Needs external check:** whether a derived rule that depends on the "complaint" (history at the start) is too easy for a learner (the discovery then costs nothing). That is a design question for the clinician and the educators.

## 4. Synthetic ECG strips and laboratory panels (DT-111)

**Outcome: Passed, with one limitation to teach around.**

* Rhythm rules (`content/ecg_findings.csv`): sinus 60–99 per minute, tachycardia 100 or more, bradycardia under 60, fast AF 120 or more, SVT 140 or more, ST elevation 1 mm or more: consistent with standard definitions. Complete heart block is defined as "under 65 per minute" (real strips peak near 30–45); the pack itself flags the class as thin (14 labelled records), so it is already presented as weak.
* ECG-08 (peaked T) and ECG-09 (flat T with U waves) are parametric synthetic strips. VR-CM-02 says a potassium of 6.5 or more that is not a faulty sample "does not sit with a normal strip". That is a **conservative consistency rule for generated cases, not a clinical fact**: real patients with severe hyperkalaemia can have a normal ECG, and the UK Kidney Association guidance does not use the ECG to exclude it. No teaching text in the case library says "a normal ECG excludes hyperkalaemia" (searched `content/debrief`, `content/decision`). Left unchanged to avoid reshaping generated cases; recorded here so no future text claims it.
* Laboratory zones (`content/generation/packs/labs.json`) were checked analyte by analyte: potassium (UKKA bands), sodium, urea, creatinine by sex, haemoglobin by sex (WHO anaemia grades), WBC, platelets, CRP, bilirubin, albumin, ALP, ALT, INR, lactate (2.0 and 4.0), glucose (6–10 peri-operative target; 4.0 and 3.0 hypoglycaemia levels), ketones (3.0 and above for ketoacidosis), eGFR grades, β-hCG, QTc by sex: **all consistent**. Lipase has no zones in the pack (no values are generated from it).
* The NEWS2 zero-score ranges used when drawing starting vitals (heart rate 51–90, systolic 111–219, respiratory rate 12–20, SpO2 96 or more, temperature 36.1–38.0) match RCP NEWS2 (2017).
* The joint laboratory model is a small-data fit (the MIMIC-IV demo, 100 patients, pairs sometimes in the tens). It is labelled as such in the pack; it is good enough to avoid unrealistic combinations and not good enough to claim real correlations.

## 5. Medication and treatment content

**Outcome: Passed, with notes.** All treatment items in `content/treatments.json` were read against the source each one cites. Verified: IV paracetamol SmPC limits, ondansetron 4 mg slow IV (PONV), 500 mL crystalloid bolus over under 15 minutes (NICE CG174 1.3.1), cefazolin 2 g (3 g at 120 kg or more), hydrocortisone 100 mg IV at induction (Association of Anaesthetists/RCP/Society for Endocrinology 2020), enoxaparin 20 or 40 mg with renal reduction, adrenaline 500 micrograms IM repeated at 5 minutes (Resuscitation Council UK; confirmed on the algorithm and the SmPC), fast-acting sugar 15–20 g and IV glucose 10 percent 150–200 mL or 20 percent 75–100 mL (JBDS/Resuscitation Council UK), the hyperkalaemia regimen (30 mL 10 percent calcium gluconate over 10 minutes, insulin 10 units in 25 g glucose, then 10 percent glucose 50 mL/hour for 5 hours if the glucose is below 7).
Notes: (a) the UK Kidney Association guideline has been revised since 2020 and the 2025 ILCOR review downgraded the evidence for IV calcium: the dose text should be re-checked against the current UKKA version by a clinician; (b) the sepsis bundle cites CG174 for the fluid bolus, which is still current; the recognition guideline is now NICE NG253 (NG51 replaced); (c) every `reviewer` field says the owner states that a clinician approves everything and the clinician is not named: that is an owner attestation, not an independent review, and this review does not change it.

## 9. Project-owner acceptance and what is still open (27 Oct 2026)
* **Acceptance.** The project owner reviewed this report and the AI-assisted research decisions and accepted the clinical content (the 13 adversarial blocks, the discovery rules, the ECG and laboratory content, the treatment and dose texts, the order descriptions) **for the educational final-year prototype**. This is the owner's acceptance for prototype development. It is **not** a clinician's review or sign-off, no clinician has signed anything, and nothing here is described as clinically approved.
* **Engineering done on the content after acceptance.** The adversarial blocks are part of the generation pipeline (`server/generation/pipeline.py` adds at most one per case, only where the case holds the anchor). The quality gate now also enforces this: `adversarial_anchor` in `server/generation/quality.py` fails a case that carries an adversarial decision whose block the library does not have or whose anchor the case does not hold (code `Q_ADVERSARIAL_ANCHOR`, tested in `tools/ci/test_generation_quality.py`), and `quality.py` no longer says that no blocks exist. The generated goldens (`tests/golden_generated`) carry the corrected blocks; the review pack and the discovery rules were regenerated with the corrections of section 3.
* **Uncertainties that remain, stated rather than resolved.** (1) ADV-07 teaches the 16 and 17 year old presumption only; there is no case with a patient under 16, so Gillick competence is not taught. (2) ADV-10 is held by SC-17 (25 years, female) and VAR-S1-K (52, female): the BSG advice differs for pre-menopausal women and the block's right option names the bowel only for older men and women past the menopause. (3) ADV-12 rests on a verbal refusal; whether that is an advance decision or a best-interests emergency is a legal as well as clinical judgement. (4) The 180/110 limit for elective surgery, the Association of Anaesthetists GLP-1 statement, the CPOC 2021 diabetes document and the current UK Kidney Association text were not verified from their primary documents. (5) VR-CM-02 (a potassium of 6.5 or more does not sit with a normal ECG) is a conservative rule for generated cases, not a clinical fact. (6) The `reviewer` fields of the treatment doses record the owner's attestation, not an independent review.
* **Separate requirement for real trainees or clinical deployment.** Before any real trainee uses the content, or any clinical use is considered, an independent licensed clinician must review and sign the items of `docs/research/clinician_signoff.md`, the institution must approve, and the privacy requirements of ADR-0027 must be met. The owner's acceptance does not satisfy any of them.
