# Public assertion-status resources: what exists, what may be used, what fits (19 October 2026)

Desk research only: nothing was downloaded, no account was created and no data use agreement was accepted. The facts below come from the pages named; every licence must be re-read on the source page before any use.

| Resource | What it labels | Access and licence (as found) | Fit for ProcSense |
|---|---|---|---|
| i2b2/VA (n2c2) 2010 assertion task | Problems in clinical notes as present, possible, absent, hypothetical, conditional or associated with someone else ([overview](https://science.ecosyste.ms/projects/53274)) | Needs an account and a signed data use agreement; third-party sharing barred; only part of the original data is released for later research ([n2c2 data sets](https://n2c2.dbmi.hms.harvard.edu/data-sets), [DUA](https://n2c2.dbmi.hms.harvard.edu/data-use-agreement)) | The label set (absent, hypothetical, someone else) matches the statuses the benchmark builds, but the units are clinical findings in discharge notes, not first-person spoken actions. Accepting a DUA is the account holder's act, not the engineer's: **not done**, recorded as an external step |
| BioScope | Negation and speculation cues and their scope in biomedical text ([paper](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2586758/), [distribution](https://rgai.inf.u-szeged.hu/node/105)) | Free for research, cite the paper; the licence text was not confirmed | Written scientific prose (abstracts, papers, radiology reports); no "present" label, no speaker. Domain mismatch is large |
| NegEx / ConText (pyConTextNLP) | Rule lists for negation, hypothetical, historical, experiencer ([pyConTextNLP](https://pypi.org/project/pyConTextNLP/0.6.1.2/)) | Open-source Python; the licence was seen only on a secondary listing (Apache-2.0), verify in the repository; the project is largely inactive (MedSpaCy succeeds it) | The cue vocabulary is a reasonable cross-check for `server/classify/assertion.py`; its triggers target findings ("no evidence of"), not "I haven't asked yet" |
| Pretrained assertion models (for example John Snow Labs, [model card](https://nlp.johnsnowlabs.com/2021/01/18/jsl_assertion_wip_en.html)) | Assertion status of entities in clinical notes | Trained on the 2010 data; commercial library, licence-gated | Not used: licence-gated, clinical-note domain, a large runtime for a 6 GB card |

Decision. None of these replaces a ProcSense-specific evaluation, and none is used. They are supplementary evidence at most: the status vocabulary of the benchmark (actual, denied, intended, hypothetical, reported, other actor, incomplete) was chosen to line up with their classes so that a person who later obtains the n2c2 data can test the status detector on it. That test is an external step (an account holder signs the agreement), listed as EX-19 in `docs/testing/SANNAN_EXTERNAL_TESTING.md`; until it exists no claim rests on public data.
