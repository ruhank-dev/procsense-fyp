# Safety of the credit path of the communication-act classifier

_Generated by `tools/bench/safety_report.py` from `reports/ai/classifier-safety.json` and `reports/ai/llm-classifier-*.json`; do not edit by hand. Every figure is SYNTHETIC and AI-judged (model-written sentences, labels by AI judges and the builder) and NOT independent: it shows how the credit path behaves on those sentences, not on real trainees. The `classifier` feature stays OFF until an independent set exists (content/ai/features.json)._

## The defect

On 18 October 2026 a fresh set of 48 evasive critical sentences (adversarial set 4) was run through the exact production path (embedding head, critical-mass cap, regular-expression cues) and **3 of 48 were credited under an ordinary act**:

| Sentence | Credited as | Head probability | Critical-act mass | Cue hit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Show me the sticker on your arm and read it aloud. | EXPLAIN_ACTION | 0.851 | 0.013 | none |
| He sipped a coffee with milk before he came. | ANSWER_QUESTION | 0.727 | 0.010 | none |
| Please come and see him within twenty minutes. | ANSWER_QUESTION | 0.585 | 0.020 | none |

## Root cause

1. **The authoritative engine was never at risk.** `EvidenceStore.Submit` refuses every critical act (CRITICAL_NEEDS_DETERMINISTIC_EVIDENCE) and the engine credits a critical item only from the action it saw. The harm was a classifier crediting an *ordinary* behaviour on a sentence that was really critical content (a wristband check, a meal, a handover request).
2. **A single 22-million-parameter embedding model read the sentences as ordinary speech.** Each got a critical-act probability of 0.010 to 0.020, below the 0.02 cap, so the critical-mass guard let them through; an unusual wording has no nearest critical neighbour in 1,940 training rows.
3. **The regular-expression cue list cannot generalise.** Version 4 of the cues (about 200 patterns) was written from the failures of earlier sets; on a new author's wording it catches only a minority (reports/ai/classifier-guard-only.json: 6 of 48 on this set). More patterns are a patch on a symptom.
4. **No second opinion and no assertion check.** One head decided alone, and a denial ("I haven't asked about allergies yet"), a hypothetical or a report ("the nurse said she would reassure you later") could be credited as the act it names.

## The fix

* **Two independent readers must agree** (`server/classify/ensemble.py`): the embedding head and the TF-IDF head (words, not meaning) must name the same ordinary act, each past its own threshold and guards. A wrong ordinary answer is rarely the same wrong answer in both.
* **Hard negatives**: the 179 evasive critical sentences of the three tuning adversarial sets are training rows of both heads with their critical act (`tools/bench/build_safe_heads.py`), so an evasive wording of an allergy, identity, consent, fasting, worsening or handover sentence lands on a critical act, which is recorded and never credited.
* **Assertion check** (`server/classify/assertion.py`): a denial, a hypothetical or a reported action earns no credit (rubric rule 10: a sentence saying the speaker has not done something is NONE).
* **Unchanged**: critical acts are never credited from speech; a down embedder credits nothing; deterministic action-based scoring stays authoritative; every rule only removes credit.

## What this does not show

* It is not validation. Authors, judges and the builder are the same model family; agreement between them is not independent evidence, and the three tuning sets are now training rows.
* The challenge set v4 was written by the same author as the earlier adversarial sets, before the gate was designed, and scored once; the assertion patterns were written after v4 existed (same author), so the v4 non-assertive row is a test of the pattern, not a held-out measure of it. Any further change needs a new frozen set (v5).
* Credit recall fell (ordinary rows credited): safety was bought with abstention. Credit is only evidence for the debrief; the menu and action path still decide.
* Nothing here turns the classifier on. `content/ai/features.json` keeps `classifier` OFF until `classifier-bench-independent.json` exists with independent authors, labellers and an adjudicator (EX-01 in docs/testing/SANNAN_EXTERNAL_TESTING.md).
