# Generative image and waveform research track: is its trigger met? (p2-ai-advanced-simulation-s04, E64)

Written 18 October 2026 from `docs/research/imaging_gaps.md` (generated by `tools/content/imaging_tags.py`) and `docs/research/dataset_register.csv`. This is the trigger analysis the step asks for before any training; no generator was trained.

## The trigger

The step starts only when there is a gap in the image library that picture **selection cannot fill**, and data to learn it from, and a free GPU.

## The gap exists

The library holds 204 pictures: 88 real and 116 synthetic. **29 findings have synthetic pictures only** (4 each, 116 in all):

| Study | Findings with synthetic pictures only |
|---|---|
| Ultrasound | 15: appendicitis, complicated appendicitis, not appendicitis, undecided, appendicitis unlikely; gallbladder with no abnormality (2 pictures sets), possible duct blockage, duct stone, less certain; pelvis (3) and kidney (2) with no abnormality |
| CT | 7: dilated small bowel, free gas, appendicitis with and without a collection, caecal tumour, diverticulitis, thickened gallbladder wall with gas |
| ECG | 3: high potassium, low potassium, new fast atrial fibrillation |
| MRCP | 2: clean duct, dilated duct with a filling defect |
| Chest X-ray | 1: free gas under the diaphragm |
| MRI | 1: appendicitis (for example in pregnancy) |

Selection cannot fill it: there is no real picture of these findings in the library to choose.

## The data to learn it from does not exist in what we may use

Of the 14 sources in the dataset register, the ones that hold real pictures are UIdataGB (gallbladder ultrasound; used), NIH ChestX-ray14 (used), AMOS 2022 (clean abdominal CT and MRI slices; used as the base the synthetic findings are painted into) and PTB-XL (ECG beat templates; used). **None holds a labelled appendicitis, duct-stone, free-gas, hyperkalaemia or MRCP-defect picture.** The one appendicitis source, the Regensburg paediatric dataset, is tabular and rejected on its non-commercial licence (DS-0008); the Indiana chest film collection is rejected on its no-derivatives licence (DS-0013). A generator cannot learn a finding it never saw, so training one on the licensed data we hold would only reproduce the clean pictures and the painted overlays we already have.

## The GPU

The 6 GB desktop card holds the 4B model for live work and is shared with the evaluations; training even a 128 to 256 pixel conditional generator needs the card free for days or a rented card with a cost recorded (the step's own plan).

## Decision

- **Trigger:** half met. The gap exists; the data to close it does not, so the track cannot start. This is the documented negative result the step's verification allows ("finding agreement at least 90 percent and rater accuracy under 70 percent, **or a documented negative result**"): not attempted, for want of licensed training data, not for want of effort.
- **What would reopen it:** a licensed dataset (open licence that allows derivatives) holding at least one of the 29 findings with labels, a named reviewer to rate real against generated pictures, and a free or rented GPU. The cheapest first step is not a generator but a search for such data; candidates found should be entered in the dataset register with their licence before anything is trained.
- **Meanwhile:** the painted findings stay labelled synthetic and practice-mode only (ADR-0003); a case that needs a real picture of one of these findings is not built.

## Repeatable check

`uv run --project tools tools/ai/adaptation_trigger.py --check` counts the 29 findings from `docs/research/imaging_gaps.md` and tests the other conditions (licensed training examples, held-out set, free card) from `content/ai/adaptation_triggers.json`; the result is `docs/research/adaptation_trigger_check.md`. It reports 'trigger not met' today and fails CI if a track ever says met without an experiment record.
