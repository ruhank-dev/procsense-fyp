# Local language model benchmarks on the development desktop

Generated by `tools/ai/bench_report.py` (p1-ai-infrastructure-s02). Do not edit by hand. Measured on **one machine**: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 SUPER, 6144 MB of video memory.

## What was measured and what was not

Done: BT0 (speed and memory), BT1 (structured output) and BT2 (grounding) for the candidate models on the desktop with nothing else heavy running, and the production-code evaluations that stand for BT4 (debrief), BT5 (composition) and BT6 (judge).
**Not done, and why:** the laptop (RTX 4050) was not available to this session; Unity (with the VR scene) and the speech recogniser were not loaded beside the model, so the shared-GPU frame-time cost is unknown; the desktop serving the laptop over the LAN was not run; BT3, the blind human rating of wording, needs people. The default model per task below is therefore a desktop-only recommendation.

## Default per task on this desktop
