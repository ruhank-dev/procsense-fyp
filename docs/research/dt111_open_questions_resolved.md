# DT-111: the open research questions, resolved with evidence

Date: 25 Oct 2026 (UTC). All imaging, ECG strips and laboratory panels named here are **synthetic**: they are teaching material built from published reference ranges and rules, not patient data and **not reviewed by a clinician** (the sign-off is tracked in `docs/research/clinician_signoff.md`).

| Question | Answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| OQ-02: is the MIMIC-IV demo (100 patients) enough for per-condition lab correlations, or should they come from published ranges and expert-set rules? | Not enough on its own for per-condition values. The shipped design takes the pairwise correlations of common analytes from the demo data (a small-data fit, labelled as such) and the per-condition values from published reference ranges and the book; drawn panels keep the fitted correlations far better than independent draws and every value stays inside its zone. | `tools/generation/fit_joint.py`, `server/generation/packs.py` (`draw_panel`), `tools/ci/test_generation_joint.py` (it is small data and says so; zones; same seed, same panel) |
| OQ-05: can PTB-XL class coverage produce hyperkalaemia ECGs, or are parametric T-wave variants needed? | Parametric variants were needed and are built: ECG-08 (peaked T waves, hyperkalaemia pattern) and ECG-09 (flat T with U waves, hypokalaemia) are synthetic strips in four renderings each (`content/ecg/ECG-08/`), tied to the potassium result by the cross-modal rule VR-CM-02: a peaked-T strip needs a high potassium in the case, a flat-T strip a low one, and a high potassium that is not a faulty sample does not sit with a normal strip. Mutations of each are caught. | `content/ecg_findings.csv` (ECG-08, SR-3107), `server/generation/crossmodal.py` (`cm02_potassium_vs_ecg`), `tools/ci/test_generation_crossmodal.py` (`test_VR_CM_02_potassium_against_the_ecg` and the mutation test) |
| OQ-03: which Piper voices may be redistributed? | Decided per voice in the licence register; only a voice marked allowed or allowed with conditions may be bundled or used to render lines. | `docs/research/licence_provenance_register.md` (MC-06, EXT-PIPER, the voices table) |
| OQ-08: Llama 3.2 3B against Qwen3.5? | Obsolete: the project uses Qwen3.5 (local, Apache-2.0) and Gemma 4 (cloud, ADR-0026); Llama is not a candidate. | `docs/decisions/ADR-0026-per-task-model-routing-ollama-cloud.md` |
| FastAPI, numpy, scipy and Mermaid licences re-read | Allowed (MIT or BSD-3-Clause), re-read on 16 Oct 2026. | `licence_provenance_register.md` (EXT-FASTAPI, EXT-SCIPY, EXT-MERMAID) |

What a clinician still has to do, and is **not** claimed here: confirm that the peaked-T strips and the potassium thresholds teach the right thing (the book's thresholds are used: SR-0109 and the hyperkalaemia rows of the stacked cases), and review the ECG renderings for realism. That sign-off is an external item, not engineering.
