# Numeric profiles: labs and vital signs

Generated by `tools/data/profile_numeric.py` (p1-dataset-analysis-s02); do not edit. Aggregates only: no patient row is kept.

**What this is.** How real values are spread in two small open datasets, so a generator can be designed against evidence. **What it is not.** A reference range (the walkthrough's bands stay the rule), a sample of healthy adults (the demo is 100 hospital patients), or anything extrapolated beyond its size. Every figure carries its n.

Sources: MIMIC-IV Clinical Database Demo 2.2 and MIMIC-IV-ED Demo 2.2 (PhysioNet, ODbL 1.0, DS-0003, DS-0004); VitalDB (DS-0001, see `vitaldb_noise.json`).
