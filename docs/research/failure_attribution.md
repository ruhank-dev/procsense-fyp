## 5. The model's own critical-content reading is an independent signal

Among the objectives the model reports (at most two per reply, fenced view so that no reply is lost), a critical objective appears for dev: 39/40 critical rows but only 1/140 legitimate rows; test: 39/40 critical rows but only 0/140 legitimate rows; test2: 35/40 critical rows but only 2/140 legitimate rows. The misses are the cases where the model read critical content as an ordinary act (the test2 allergy question): exactly the survivors above. A reading that finds NO critical objective is therefore not evidence that the content is not critical, but one that finds one is strong evidence that it is.

## 7. What this does and does not show

The variant results use the model's recorded readings of the benchmark sentences and the head's signals for them; the conditions were read off the analysis of dev, test and test2 and were not tuned on a held-out set, so they are exploratory. The 8 sentences that leak when the cap is removed from the safety panel (`reports/ai/recall-safety-panel.json`: fasting, blood thinner, "initial each box", senior anaesthetist...) all have the ordinary act outweighing the critical mass (ratio 1.9 to 10), so the dominance condition does not stop them: only the model's critical reading could, and the model was never asked about them (it needs a small live run). Zero observed unsafe offers on synthetic rows is not a safety guarantee.
