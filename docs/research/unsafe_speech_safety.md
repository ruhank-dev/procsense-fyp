# Unsafe-speech safety evaluation (generated)

> Generated by `tools/bench/unsafe_speech_report.py` from the committed result files; do not edit. **Synthetic, builder-written sentences with provisional labels: none of this is independent validation** (EX-01 and DT-231/DT-232 stay open; `speech_interpretation` stays OFF). Decisions and their reasons: ADR-0023; the layers and their gaps: `safety_layers_audit.md`; the review protocol: `docs/testing/safety_challenge_review.md`.

## 1. What was evaluated

Every row is built from an approved-style seed family by one transformation template (`tools/bench/safety_seeds.py`, `metamorph.py`); each row records its seed, its template and the outcome the construction implies. `credit_allowed` = a performed, non-critical act in its window (the system may offer it); `no_credit` = speech that must never become credited evidence; `ambiguous_review` = the construction does not settle the label (excluded from pass or fail, listed for a person). Seed families and templates are disjoint across the three splits (crossing: 0 families, 0 templates); 23 generated rows that were near-copies (word overlap 0.8 or more) of a `tests/action_bench_v1` sentence or of a development sentence were dropped.

| split | rows | credit_allowed | no_credit | ambiguous_review | role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dev | 1059 | 106 | 841 | 112 | development: the implementation was changed on its failures |
| challenge | 507 | 70 | 395 | 42 | first look recorded in section 4, then used for development (no longer independent evidence) |
| final | 513 | 65 | 420 | 28 | untouched by any change; each configuration scored once (the candidate was frozen first, then scored) |

Review status: 267 rows are PENDING_HUMAN_REVIEW (every ambiguous or clinically relevant row), 1812 AUTO_PROVISIONAL. No person has labelled any of them.

Both configurations were run by `tools/bench/safety_eval.py` on the same rows, the same embeddings and the same engine build.

## 4. Does fixing the development failures generalise? The first look at the challenge split

The challenge split was written before the implementation was changed and was looked at once, after the changes that the DEVELOPMENT failures called for (no challenge sentence was consulted for them). The first two rows were measured by tools/bench/safety_eval.py on the working tree at those two moments (the baseline row is reproducible from baseline-4a2ed00-challenge.json; the first-pass row is a recorded figure whose raw file was not retained because the tree changed afterwards). The third row is reproducible from s16-challenge.json but is NOT independent: the mechanisms behind the 40 remaining failures were generalised after looking at them.

| step | unsafe credit on the challenge split | legitimate credited |

## 7. What this does and does not establish

* **Measured:** counts above, on the rows above, by the engine's own evidence store; the committed raw predictions in `reports/ai/safety/*-raw.json` let every number be recomputed.
* **Estimated only:** nothing here estimates real-world rates. The seeds and the templates were written by one author (the builder, a language model), the labels follow from the construction, and the development rows were used to change the code.
* **Not established:** independent validation (no human has labelled these rows; EX-01), clinical acceptance, headset behaviour (DC-08, DC-09, Sannan), real learner speech, real recogniser output beyond the small noise templates, and any claim that unsafe speech is now safe. A recogniser that drops a negation (`asr_negation_lost`) produces a transcript no text rule can refuse; the only defences are recogniser confidence, an audio-level check, or the verified action itself.
