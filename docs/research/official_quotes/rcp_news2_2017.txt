Recommendations (report page xx): new confusion scores 3 on the NEWS chart; the AVPU term has been amended to ACVPU, where C represents new confusion.
Scale 2 is for patients with hypercapnic respiratory failure and a recommended oxygen saturation range of 88–92%, confirmed on blood gas analysis, decided by a competent clinical decision maker and recorded in the notes.
