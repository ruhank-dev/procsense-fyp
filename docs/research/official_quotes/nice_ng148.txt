NICE NG148 Acute kidney injury: prevention, detection and management (opened 10 Oct 2026, https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng148/chapter/Recommendations)

1.3.1 Detect acute kidney injury, in line with the (p)RIFLE (paediatric Risk, Injury, Failure, Loss, End stage renal disease), AKIN (Acute Kidney Injury Network) or KDIGO (Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes) definitions, by using any of the following criteria:
- a rise in serum creatinine of 26 micromol/litre or greater within 48 hours
- a 50% or greater rise in serum creatinine known or presumed to have occurred within the past 7 days
- a fall in urine output to less than 0.5 ml/kg/hour for more than 6 hours in adults and more than 8 hours in children and young people
- a 25% or greater fall in eGFR in children and young people within the past 7 days.

KDIGO 2012 (Section 2.1.1 and the staging table, as reproduced by the Merck Manual and several PMC tables): stage 1 = 1.5 to 1.9 times baseline OR an increase of 0.3 mg/dL (26.5 micromol/L) or more; the 0.3 mg/dL rise is judged within 48 hours, the ratio against a baseline known or presumed within the prior 7 days.
