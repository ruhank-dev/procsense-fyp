* Nearest development neighbour of each challenge row (word overlap): median 0.286, 95th percentile 0.429, maximum 0.714; 1 of 507 rows at 0.6 or more, 1 at 0.7 or more (a wrapper shared in kind, such as a negation, with a different seed).
* Nearest development neighbour of each final row (word overlap): median 0.273, 95th percentile 0.455, maximum 0.714; 5 of 513 rows at 0.6 or more, 1 at 0.7 or more (a wrapper shared in kind, such as a negation, with a different seed).
* Wording frames (the template's own words with the seed replaced): identical frames shared between dev and challenge 1, dev and final 1, challenge and final 1.
* challenge: 1 of 26 frames have word overlap 0.6 or more with some development frame (median 0.333). Independence of *seed and template identity* holds; independence of *wording style* does not: the same author wrote all three, and several final frames are paraphrases of development frames (for example a negation in other words).

## 6. What this audit can and cannot show

Can: that the sets are reproducible, that no seed family or template crosses a split, that categories are covered unevenly, where labels are fragile by construction. Cannot: that any label is right, that the final split is independent of the development split in *style* (one author), or that the rows resemble trainee speech. The final split is untouched (its hash is pinned in `tests/safety_challenge/frozen.json`); it is spent for every configuration after `s16`, so a later fix needs a fresh final set.
