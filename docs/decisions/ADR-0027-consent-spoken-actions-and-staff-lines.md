# ADR-0027 — Cloud processing of trainee speech needs the person's own recorded consent; spoken questions start the same simulator actions as the tablet; the synthetic staff lines are approved for practice

**Date:** 25 October 2026 · **Status:** Accepted (owner decisions of 25 Oct 2026 on DT-248, DT-251 and DT-252). The consent flow, the spoken actions and the line approval are implemented and tested. Real trainee speech still reaches Ollama Cloud only when the operator records the institutional approval AND the person has consented. · **Related:** ADR-0005, ADR-0025, ADR-0026, DT-127, DT-248, DT-251, DT-252

## Context
* The owner approved Ollama Cloud for ProcSense's AI features, including trainee speech and dialogue, subject to an informed-consent and privacy flow in the sign-up, and asked that spoken questions work as an alternative to the tablet, with the existing Gemma evaluator connected to the Unity speech pipeline and no change to ADR-0025.
* What the provider says (read on 25 Oct 2026, https://ollama.com/privacy and https://docs.ollama.com/cloud): prompts and responses are processed "transiently" and "never" used for training; content "is not stored beyond the time required to fulfill the request"; diagnostic metadata excludes prompt and response content; data "may be transferred to and processed in the United States"; third parties include "cloud infrastructure providers, model inference providers". Not stated: a retention period, the names of the hosts or subprocessors, where the models run. The notice repeats these statements as the provider's, says what could not be verified, and promises nothing more.
* Until now only the absence of a Unity caller kept real speech away from the cloud evaluator.

## Decisions
1. **Documents.** `content/legal/terms.json` (Terms and Conditions) and `content/legal/privacy_notice.json` (Privacy Notice) are two separate, versioned documents, public at `GET /legal/terms` and `GET /legal/privacy-notice`, read from the sign-up page and from the Privacy page. They are plain-language drafts written from what the service does; they have not been reviewed by a lawyer and claim no legal compliance. The fields only the deploying institution can fill are marked `[institution]` (DT-127).
2. **Questions.** Four answers, each recorded with the document version, the time, the fingerprint (SHA-256) of the exact question and documents shown, and where it was given (only the web can answer): `terms` (required: accepts the Terms, and says so in its text that it is not consent to anything optional), `results` (required, as before), `research` (optional), and the new `cloud` (optional, unticked by default, points at the Privacy Notice). A new text version asks everyone again. A person can change or withdraw any answer on the Privacy page; the export shows every answer with its fingerprint. Migration 0009 adds the two columns and the two kinds.
3. **What leaves the service, as the notice states it.** Voice is recorded only while Talk is held (at most 15 s), recognised on the ProcSense service by its own recogniser, not stored, never sent to a cloud provider. With the cloud consent, the transcript, up to four earlier lines, the facts the patient may say now, the list of available actions and a scenario identifier go to Ollama Cloud (Gemma 4 31B). No name, email, account identifier or audio. Our audit row keeps hashes and reason codes, no text.
4. **Enforcement** (`server/llm/routing.py`): the `trainee`-class routes (patient dialogue, staff, the spoken-action interpreter) and the speech evaluator use a cloud model only when cloud routing is on AND either the deployment is declared synthetic (invented data), or the operator has recorded the institutional approval (`CLOUD_TRAINEE_DATA_APPROVED`) AND this person's latest `cloud` answer is yes to the current text. The routes read the person's stored answer for each request; accepting the Terms, a no, no answer, a withdrawal or an older text all mean the local model. The evaluator route answers UNAVAILABLE with the menu in that case, as before for a feature that is off. Consent without the institutional approval opens nothing. `GET /ai/health` shows the state without any secret.
5. **Spoken actions** (`POST /speech/organisations/{id}/action`, `prompts/speech/action_v1.txt`, `content/ai/spoken_actions.json`): the model reads the transcript, the ids of the tablet actions enabled now and the last four turns and returns up to two ids from a closed list built on the server, and who was addressed. No keyword classifier and no checker model: a role, a situation, how to treat denials, plans, hypotheticals, retractions and follow-ups, examples and a strict schema. The list holds only conversational actions (introduce, ask identity, ask allergies, the twelve history topics, summarise, pain score, ask the nurse for a wristband). Physical and safety-critical actions (confirm identity, report a mismatch, read the wristband, open notes, attach or measure) are not in it, so speech cannot earn credit for them.
6. **Same path as the tablet.** Unity starts a chosen action with `TabletView.PressId`, the call a button press makes, so the engine decides what is allowed, what changes and what is scored. PlayMode proves the state hash and the log are byte-identical to the tapped action. A repeated recognition of the same sentence inside four seconds is dropped. The engine's refusal is shown and the patient does not answer. An ask that is a history topic is answered by the patient through the dialogue; one that is not (allergies, reason, home support) is answered with the case's authored line. Follow-ups use the conversation so far. The nurse's observations are the monitor's values from the engine's state, never a model's words. The existing evaluator (`gemma-eval-5`, unchanged) judges the same sentence for communication credit once per utterance id and applies it as before.
7. **The local model only proposes.** Measured on the same set, the cloud model started exactly the right actions in 100% of 106 trials with 0% false actions; the local Qwen 90.6% with 10% false actions (for example "Anaesthetist, come to the bedside" started the wristband request). A local result is shown as "Did you mean ...?" with a confirm button and starts nothing by itself. A decline of the cloud consent therefore costs convenience, not correctness.
8. **Staff lines.** `content/ai/staff_intent_lines.json` is approved for educational practice use on the owner's project-level authorization (status `approved_for_practice_synthetic`, provenance kept: engineer-authored synthetic text). It is not independent clinician validation and not approval for assessment (staff speak in practice only). Staff speak only when addressed or called for (`stay_silent`, ADR-0026).
9. **Speech recognition on the card.** The service now loads the card libraries by PATH as well as by directory, and when the card fails to compute it recognises the clip again on the processor once and stays there. Measured on this machine (GTX 1660 SUPER, `small.en`): 734 ms median on the card against 7.7 s on the processor for the same eight synthetic clips, the same words on both.

## Alternatives
* **Treat the Terms as the consent:** rejected (the owner asked for a separate, affirmative, recorded consent).
* **Consent per request or only a deployment switch:** rejected; a personal, withdrawable, versioned answer is what was asked and is what the routes read.
* **Keyword or classifier mapping from speech to actions:** rejected (ADR-0025 philosophy); the interpreter is a prompt and a schema.
* **Let a local model start actions by itself:** rejected on the measured 10% false actions.
* **Let speech start physical actions (confirm identity, attach a sensor):** rejected; it would let words earn credit for a physical act.

## Consequences
* A real deployment enables cloud processing for consenting people only after (a) a qualified person has reviewed the Terms and the Privacy Notice and the institution has completed its fields and agreed (DT-127, DT-248: external), and (b) the operator sets `CLOUD_TRAINEE_DATA_APPROVED`. Until then those people use the local model. Development and demonstration deployments stay on `CLOUD_SYNTHETIC_DEPLOYMENT` with invented data only.
* A spoken question for a topic the engine has not released is answered by the engine's rules, not by a model: a refusal is shown. The `ask` action is the only thing that releases a history fact.
* The interpreter's list and the staff lines are `awaiting_review` for assessment use; clinical or assessment validation stays separate (EX-13).

## Revisit if
* The provider's policy changes (re-read it and bump the notice version), a qualified person asks for different wording, human ratings or a fresh set disagree with the measured accuracy (DT-250), or Quest testing finds spoken actions confusing (DC-07).
