# ADR-0023 — Unsafe, non-assertive and out-of-context speech is made visible to measurement, refused by named layers, and every critical check is shown by mutation to be needed

**Date:** 20 October 2026 · **Status:** Accepted for the engineering (owner request of 20 Oct 2026, prompt 176; step p1-ai-language-s16). Independent validation, clinical acceptance and Quest acceptance are NOT DONE; `speech_interpretation` stays OFF.

## Context
* After the recall follow-up (ADR-0022 addendum, commit `4a2ed00`) the interpretation path accepted more legitimate speech, with the critical-mass cap at 0.05 and the engine's thresholds calibrated. The owner asked whether it is also robust to a broad range of unsafe, misleading, non-assertive, ambiguous and out-of-context speech, and for evidence of generalisation rather than a longer list of hand-written counterexamples.
* A mutation (disabling `validate`) had not been caught by the recall tests. The audit (`docs/research/safety_layers_audit.md`) found why: the guards overlap by design, so the final decision cannot show which guard did the work.
* All earlier figures are synthetic, builder-written and not independent.

## Decision
1. **Measure first, on rows with provenance.** `tests/safety_challenge` (2,079 rows in `dev`, `challenge`, `final`) is generated by `tools/bench/metamorph.py` from seed families (`tools/bench/safety_seeds.py`) and transformation templates (negation and its scope, hypothetical, future, unfinished action, another person, quotation, retraction, question and advice, hedge, fragment, combinations, critical claims, out-of-window, no waiting context, duplicate utterance and behaviour, contentless utterance, false completion, ASR-style noise). Every row records its seed, template and expected outcome (`credit_allowed`, `no_credit`, `ambiguous_review`); labels are by construction and PROVISIONAL; every ambiguous or clinically relevant row is `PENDING_HUMAN_REVIEW`. Seed families and templates never cross a split; near-copies of `tests/action_bench_v1` and of development rows are dropped. The taxonomy (`tests/safety_challenge/taxonomy.json`) names for each category the expected decision, the safeguards, the residual risk and the tests.
2. **Count what the engine accepts.** `tools/bench/safety_eval.py` runs each row through the interpretation path and every offer through the engine's own `EvidenceStore` (`evidence-check`, extended with sessions and utterance ids), keeps raw predictions and rejection reasons, records the configuration (file hashes, version, cap, model, commit), scores the final split once per configuration, and runs the unchanged `4a2ed00` on the identical rows.
3. **Fix mechanisms, not examples.** The assertion layer (`server/classify/assertion.py`) gains named kinds (`quoted`, `retracted`, `meta_question`, `hedged`, `contentless`, and broader `denied`, `intended`, `other_actor`, `reported`, `hypothetical`, `incomplete`), found by development failures and then generalised after a first look at the challenge split showed that the first pass over-fitted wordings (99 percent of development failures removed, 38 percent of challenge failures). Every pattern only removes credit; `tools/bench/assertion_diff.py --before 4a2ed00` shows no legitimate sentence of any set newly flagged. The critical-mass cap (0.05) and every threshold are unchanged.
4. **The engine re-checks what only the client knew.** `SpeechEvidenceSubsystem` now rejects `ANSWER_QUESTION` evidence when the session holds no waiting patient question, and `CLOSED_LOOP` when it holds no critical result awaiting read-back (the same definitions as the interpretation context). Before this, the store had no such check and the service trusted the context it was sent.
5. **Refusals are logged by cause, without words.** The audit row carries the rule-set version (`INTERPRET_VERSION`, in `prompt_id`) and the reason codes of every decision; a refusal is `validation: failed` with its codes (`reason_codes()`), an offer is `passed`; no utterance text is stored (the existing hash rule is unchanged). A failing classifier is the menu (`safe_interpret`); a cancellation is not swallowed.
6. **Every critical check is shown to be needed.** `tools/ci/mutation_safety.py` disables each guard in a copy of the code (the edit must match exactly once), runs `tools/ci/safety_scenarios.py` (a stub gate that credits at full confidence in front of the guard under test, layer scenarios, the engine's store on direct offers) and requires the outcome to change; double mutants remove every service copy of a barrier, engine mutants remove one store or subsystem check. `tools/ci/test_mutation_safety.py` pins the results.
7. **Independent validation is prepared, not claimed.** `docs/testing/safety_challenge_review.md` (EX-01 part B): a blind review sheet, roles, disagreement resolution, frozen configuration, metrics and acceptance criteria. The existing thresholds (DT-229) are untouched and PENDING; the additions proposed there are PENDING and wired to no gate.

## Alternatives
* **More hand-written counterexamples only:** rejected, they were the cause of the false assurance (the fixes that make each pass need not generalise; the first-look result shows they did not).
* **Raise recall again by loosening the cap or an assertion check:** not done and not needed to answer the question; the cap is the head's only defence against a critical statement in words no cue covers (8 of 9 cap-only sentences are accepted by the engine without it).
* **A learned non-assertion classifier or another model:** no confirmed failure that a targeted experiment could change; deferred until independent data exists (EX-01). The assertion kinds are rules, which is also why they are bypassable by unlisted wordings.
* **Mutating the working tree in place:** rejected; mutants run in copies.

## Consequences
* On the final split (no change made after or because of seeing it): unsafe credit fell from 41 of 407 (10.1 percent, 95 percent CI 7.5 to 13.4) to 10 of 407 (2.5 percent, CI 1.3 to 4.5), with legitimate credit unchanged (36 of 65) and no error, timeout or malformed output. On development 126 of 828 to 0 and on challenge 64 of 385 to 0, **but those splits were used to change the code**. The benchmark `tests/action_bench_v1` is unchanged (95/140, 76/140, 79/140).
* The remaining final-split failures (10) are named in `docs/research/unsafe_speech_safety.md`; they are *not fixed*, because fixing them against the split that revealed them would end its use as a final set. They are DT-233.
* Not mitigable by text: a recogniser that drops a negation (`asr_negation_lost`); a negation whose scope is a reason (`ambiguous_review`).
* Speaker attribution remains lexical; the Unity client is not mutation-run (its 8 EditMode tests stand).
* The rule set is versioned (`INTERPRET_VERSION = interpret-2026-10-20.2`). Rollback of the assertion changes is `git revert` of the commit; the cap and thresholds were not touched.

## Evidence and what it cannot show
Synthetic, one author (the builder, a language model), labels by construction, no human review: this is evidence that known mechanisms are closed and measurable, and an *optimistic* estimate of behaviour on unseen speech. It is not independent validation (EX-01, DT-231), not clinical acceptance and not headset acceptance (Sannan, DC-08 and DC-09, NOT RUN). Zero failures on a finite synthetic set, or on a set used for selection, is not zero real-world risk.
