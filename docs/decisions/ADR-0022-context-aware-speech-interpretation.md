# ADR-0022 — Spoken communication is interpreted against the real scenario state, validated deterministically and only then offered to the engine as evidence

**Date:** 19 October 2026 · **Status:** Accepted for the engineering (owner request of 19 Oct 2026, prompt 174); IMPLEMENTED the same day (steps s10 to s14, engineering complete and tested; headset and independent evidence outstanding); the `speech_interpretation` gate is OFF until its acceptance passes · **Related:** ADR-0005, ADR-0011, ADR-0016, ADR-0020, ADR-0021; steps p1-ai-language-s10 to s14, p1-voice-input-s02; features C31, C36, C37, C24, E55

## Context
* The engine already has the authority half: `EvidenceStore.Submit` (`src/ProcSense.Core/Scoring/Evidence.cs`) records speech evidence, never credits a critical act, applies the per-act threshold and **rejects an act whose behaviour window (`content/assessment/mapping.json` `steps`) does not contain the trainee's step** (`CONTEXT_MISMATCH`). Nothing outside the tests calls it: no speech reaches the session log, and the debrief sees deterministic evidence only.
* The classifier route (`server/routers/classify.py`) receives the sentence alone. No scenario state, step, recent turn or pending question reaches it, so the context checks that the engine would make are never prepared, and ambiguous pairs that context separates (an answer to the patient's pending question against an explanation; a read-back against a fact noted back) cannot be separated.
* Measured, all on the synthetic `tests/action_bench_v1` (one author family, labels by construction, NOT independent):
  * the retained gate (ADR-0020: hybrid head, TF-IDF agreement, critical cues, widened assertion check) credits 0/40 critical and 0/300 non-assertive sentences on test2 with 69/140 legitimate recall at 69/72 precision, in about 15 ms;
  * zero-shot NLI (BART-large-MNLI) is far worse (`docs/research/nli_zero_shot.md`);
  * the Qwen 3.5 4B rubric evaluator, one call per utterance over all 19 objectives, credited 6/40 critical sentences on test2 even after the production guards, recalled 67/140, and needs 3.4 s median and 4.1 s p95 (`docs/research/rubric_evaluator.md`). The voice round trip has 0.79 s left between recognition and the first spoken sentence (C31 target 4 s p95), the live-call timeout is 3 s, and one evaluator call delays a patient reply from 0.6 s to 3.9 s on the single model slot (ADR-0011).
* That evaluator did not receive real per-row context: the benchmark rows carry no scenario state. Context conditioning is therefore untested, not refuted.

## Decision
1. **Flow.** Transcript + actual scenario context → semantic candidates → deterministic validation → decision (offer as evidence / clarify / abstain / menu) → the engine's `EvidenceStore` decides acceptance → approved scoring and the debrief. The model and the classifier only ever propose.
2. **Context is built by the engine, from real state, and typed** (step s10). `InterpretationContext` (C#, `src/ProcSense.Core/Speech/`): scenario and case id, mode, the workflow steps of the running flow stages, the objectives eligible at those steps (behaviour windows of `mapping.json`, computed deterministically in the engine), the acts already accepted (duplicate prevention), whether the patient has a question pending (consent subsystem) and whether a colleague's instruction is waiting for a read-back, the last turns with their speakers, and the addressee. Nothing hidden (facts the trainee has not earned, expected answers, scores) is in it. **An objective outside the window is not eligible** (the engine's existing rule); there is no out-of-order exception in the repository, and none is invented.
3. **Candidate generator = the retained gate (ADR-0020), restricted to the eligible objectives** (step s11). The hybrid head's ranked acts are filtered by the eligible list; the TF-IDF agreement, the critical-mass cap, the critical cues and the assertion check stay as they are. Status (actual, question, negated, intended, hypothetical, reported, other actor, incomplete) is a deterministic classification next to it. Qwen 4B is **not** in the live path. It remains an offline experiment (`tools/bench/rubric_eval.py`) that may be re-run only on the independent set with real context, and only as a debrief-time aid if it then beats the gate.
4. **Deterministic validation outside any model** (step s11): the act must be eligible now, non-critical, asserted (status actual), have a span in the transcript, not already be accepted for the same behaviour in this session, and come from the trainee. The model's or classifier's own confidence never overrides a rejection.
5. **Uncertainty is answered, not guessed** (step s12): at most one focused clarification question per utterance (the two candidates named in plain words), then the menu of the eligible objectives' existing actions; failure of a service (timeout, malformed output, unavailable model, cancelled request) gives the menu and no credit. A critical topic is never credited from speech: the reply points to the action (wristband, form, checklist) that the engine verifies.
6. **Speech evidence enters the session as a logged, replayable entry** (step s10): `speech_evidence` is applied through `Session.Apply` like any action, so replay reproduces it, and `EvidenceStore.FromLog` submits it through the same checks. It can map only to `ai_acts`, never to a behaviour's `actions`: procedural and physical credit still needs the engine-verified action.
7. **Patient conversation stays in its own pipeline** (step s13): the grounded dialogue (`server/dialogue/dialogue.py`) answers; its text is never fed back as trainee speech, never mutates assessment state and triggers no action; injection, off-topic and unsupported clinical questions get the approved fallback; one retry, bounded length.
8. **Gate.** A new feature gate `speech_interpretation` (OFF) with suites read by `tools/eval/ai_gate.py`; the proposed thresholds are PENDING the owner's approval (step s14). Speech-based scoring stays OFF until every suite passes on the independent set (EX-01).

## Alternatives
* Qwen 4B rubric evaluator in the live path: 4.1 s p95, 6/40 critical credited on test2 with guards; rejected for the live path (`reports/ai/rubric-eval-frozen-test2.json`, `reports/ai/rubric-latency.json`).
* Zero-shot NLI: core accuracy 0.26 to 0.39; rejected (`reports/ai/nli-zero-shot.json`).
* Out-of-window acts allowed with a flag (as the rubric experiment did): contradicts the engine's approved rule; rejected.
* A new classifier trained now: no independent data to show it beats the gate; deferred to E60 and EX-01.

## Consequences
The live path stays fast (classifier about 15 ms plus validation) and model-independent; context removes a class of errors deterministically, not by keyword patches. New work: steps p1-ai-language-s10 to s14, the voice-client half of p1-voice-input-s02 (Sannan's Quest test), DT-226 to DT-230. Recall stays limited by the gate (about half of the legitimate synthetic sentences); the menu remains the guaranteed path.

## Revisit if
The independent set (EX-01) exists and a candidate (context-aware Qwen offline, or E60) beats the gate there on critical false credit, legitimate recall and credited precision; a faster local runtime brings a model call under 0.5 s p95; the owner approves an out-of-window rule.

## Outcome of the implementation (19 October 2026)
Built: the engine context and speech evidence in the log (s10), the `/interpret` service (s11), clarification, menu and failure handling with the Unity script (s12), dialogue safety (s13), the context-conditioned benchmark and the gate (s14). Measured on the synthetic `tests/action_bench_v1` (preliminary, not independent; `docs/research/interpretation_benchmark.md`): the path offers exactly what the classifier route offers after the engine's window rule (legitimate recall unchanged, 66 of 140 on test and 69 of 140 on test2, credited precision 66 of 66 and 69 of 69) and removes the route's wrong credits without state (4 of 70 and 3 of 72); no critical, non-assertive or out-of-window offer; median 16 ms, p95 30 ms per interpretation. **Context did not raise recall**: the misses are the gate's critical-mass cap. Whether context helps on human-written dialogue is not measured and needs the independent set (EX-01).
