## 1. Intended user experience

Occasionally a case tests a known weak spot with a fair trap: a similar-named patient, a misleading handover, an allergy only in old notes. The debrief shows the cue the trainee could have used.

## 2. What exists and what is missing

**Exists in the repository:**

- hand-made distractors (DS-0012)
- VAR-S1-J haemolysed potassium

**Missing:**

- trap block catalogue
- trap selection from weaknesses
- AI-written misleading wording within fairness rules

**Deterministic foundation:** Each trap must have a discoverable cue and resolution (fairness rule) checked deterministically.
