## 1. Intended user experience

What the trainee says is understood in its context and the right communication earns graded credit in the debrief only when the trainee actually did it now, at the right moment of the scenario; an unclear or unavailable reading falls back to the menu; nothing critical is ever credited from speech.

## 2. What exists and what is missing

**Exists in the repository:**

- Gemma semantic evaluator with a strict contract, bounded retry and fail-safe (server/evaluate, route POST /evaluate/organisations/{id}, gate speech_evaluation, ON since 22 Oct 2026)
- engine judgement checks and graded communication score (Evidence.cs, CommunicationScorer.cs, ScoreReport.Communication)
- Unity SpeechEvaluation script (request, parse, apply as speech_evidence; EditMode-tested, not in a scene)
- development set of 493 items and two controlled partial-credit sets (synthetic)
- layered evaluation tool for the independent set (tools/bench/ex01_eval.py)
- fresh held-out sets v1 and v2 and a fresh partial-credit set v3, each scored once with criteria fixed beforehand (tools/synth_heldout, tools/synth_heldout_v2, tools/synth_partial_v3; reports/ai/speech-evaluation-*.json)
- gate acceptance record (tools/eval/speech_acceptance.py, reports/ai/speech-evaluation-acceptance.json)

**Missing:**

- real Meta Quest and ASR end-to-end validation (DC-09 and DC-10, Sannan)
- the transcript privacy decision before real trainee speech is sent to Ollama Cloud (DT-248)
- taxonomy boundary decisions (DT-241 to DT-244)

**Deterministic foundation:** Eligibility from the behaviour windows of content/assessment/mapping.json, the performed-now check, waiting question and read-back, credit floor, duplicate check, critical exclusion and the graded formula of content/scoring/rubric.json, all in the engine (EvidenceStore is the only authority).

## 3. The AI in this feature (seven questions)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| 1. What is the AI supposed to do? | Read what the sentence does in its scene and grade it: who, when, assertion, objective(s), relevance, correctness, completeness, physical claim. |
| 2. Why is AI useful here? | Meaning depends on negation, tense, modality and speaker; word lists and embeddings lost legitimate credit and were patched by layers. |
| 3. Which method for which part? | Gemma judges meaning (strict JSON); the engine decides what the judgement is worth from its own state. |
| 4. Deterministic parts still needed | State, window, critical exclusion, physical verification, duplicates, arithmetic. |
| 5a. AI already implemented | Gemma evaluator and the engine graded scoring, approved 22 Oct 2026, gate ON. |
| 5b. AI missing | Real-device (Quest and ASR) validation, DC-10. |
| 6. What we must build | Nothing further for the software acceptance; scene wiring and the headset check are Sannan. |
| 7. How we prove it beats a simple baseline | Fewer false credits (8 against 26 items on the same development set) at comparable recall, without keyword guards. |
