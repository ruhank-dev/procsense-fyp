## 1. Intended user experience

The trainee asks the patient anything, by voice or text; the patient answers in character, tells what they know when asked well, hesitates on sensitive topics until trust is earned, and never reveals what the rules say they would not.

## 2. What exists and what is missing

**Exists in the repository:**

- fixed history questions with quoted answers
- disclosure gate and trust model
- per-role read-only context
- grounding validator
- 100+ disclosure probes

**Missing:**

- served model
- persona prompt
- answer generation
- evaluation set of free questions

**Deterministic foundation:** Disclosure gate decides what may be said; context never holds hidden facts.
