## 1. Intended user experience

The patient is one continuous body and person: their physiology responds to what the trainee does, their state carries into a follow-up session, and they describe how they feel consistently with that state.

## 2. What exists and what is missing

**Exists in the repository:**

- LitePhysio with couplings and display zones
- PULSE spike planned (p1-pulse-physiology)

**Missing:**

- persistent twin state across sessions
- symptom description from state
- generated-profile initialisation

**Deterministic foundation:** Physiology is deterministic and replayable.
