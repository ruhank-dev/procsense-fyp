## 1. Intended user experience

During practice a hint appears when the trainee is stuck; between sessions a short coaching note says what to focus on next and why.

## 2. What exists and what is missing

**Exists in the repository:**

- deterministic hints (HintTexts, simulation-workflow s05)

**Missing:**

- coaching notes from patterns
- adaptive hint wording

**Deterministic foundation:** Rules choose when and which hint.
