# p1-ai-language-s20 — EX-01 phase two: kit and layered evaluation tool for an independent human-labelled evaluation of the semantic reader (tooling only; no data, no labels, no run)

Milestone: **M10 P1 platform — backend, AI, voice input**

## What
Owner requests of 21 October 2026 (prompts 183 and 184): stop optimising against dev, test and test2, keep the valid configuration (`cap_policy=hard`, both switches OFF, thresholds, cue and engine unchanged, the verifier experimental only), and prepare the next phase, an independent evaluation, so that the first run against it is a genuine measurement.

1. **Plan** (`docs/testing/ex01_semantic_evaluation_plan.md`): roles and independence rules, the 17 coverage families (`tools/bench/ex01_design.json`), sample-size arithmetic (Wilson), procedure and commands, what the run reports, what it cannot show, the owner's decisions.
2. **Kit** (`tools/bench/ex01_kit.py`): author brief with no example sentence, coverage and leak checks, a ban on invented ASR typos (real-recording reference required), blind annotator files (scene and sentence only, own order), agreement and kappa, adjudication, disagreements kept and never forced, `definition_insufficient` items routed to a specification-issue list, a freeze that refuses unless different non-builder people filled the roles, with SHA-256 manifest and `verify`.
3. **Layered evaluation tool** (`tools/bench/ex01_eval.py`): (1) reader understanding before any gate, (2) final credited output after every gate, (3) shipped baseline, (4) experimental verifier without any cap lift; recall, precision, confusion by objective, critical and adversarial false credits, abstentions, menus, clarification opportunities, schema compliance, latency, component of each miss, and an attribution of understanding against conservative downstream rejection. It refuses an unfrozen set, a configuration not frozen first or changed after, and a second scored run.
4. **Annotator and adjudicator guide** and the generated templates (`docs/testing/ex01_templates/`).
5. **Tests**: `tools/ci/test_ex01_kit.py` (18) and `server/tests/test_ex01_eval.py` (16), placeholder fixtures only.

6. **Owner decisions of prompt 184**: 300 items, risk-weighted over the 17 families (164 safety-sensitive; F09 and F14 weighted most); real-ASR family F17 at 0 and refused by the kit, no invented typos, ASR evaluated in a separate later phase; every target reported as a point estimate AND a 95 percent Wilson interval with the lower-bound claim kept apart and zero counts reported with an exact upper bound; author packages for two authors (`docs/testing/ex01_author_packages/`, pre-structured, no sentence); `combine`, near-copy leak check (repository sentence never shown) and the `summary` command for the owner's pre-freeze review; the independence designs (strict five-person S, four-person cross-annotation A, three-person B not recommended) in `docs/testing/ex01_role_designs.md`. Per-item role enforcement for design A is NOT implemented and waits for the owner's choice.

## Why
The synthetic sets have shaped the architecture, prompts, guards and verifier too heavily for more tuning to be convincing evidence. The 90 percent target can only be demonstrated on data nobody on the build side has seen.
