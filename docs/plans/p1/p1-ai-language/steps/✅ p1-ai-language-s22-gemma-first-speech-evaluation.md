# p1-ai-language-s22 — Gemma semantic evaluation with engine-side graded scoring replaces the classifier, cap and gate stack (ADR-0025)

Milestone: **M10 P1 platform — backend, AI, voice input**

## What
Owner decision of 21 October 2026: the target architecture is ASR, then Gemma semantic evaluation, then authoritative simulator state and evidence, then a deterministic score calculation, then VR feedback.

1. **Gemma evaluator** (`server/evaluate/`, route `POST /evaluate/organisations/{id}`, `gemma4:31b` on the free plan of Ollama Cloud): a prompt (`gemma-eval-4`) that forces the semantic order (who, when, assertion and modality, objective only if performed by the trainee, every justified objective, relevance/correctness/completeness 0 to 3, physical claims never assumed), carries the approved annotation rules verbatim and original contrast examples, and a strict output contract with a bounded retry and a menu fail-safe. The route returns a judgement, never a score.
2. **Engine side** (`src/ProcSense.Core`): each judged objective is applied as a `speech_evidence` action; `EvidenceStore` accepts it only if the trainee performed it now, a step is running, the behaviour window and the waiting question or read-back fit, the grade clears the credit floor and the utterance was not already judged; a critical objective is graded for a small communication credit and never accepted as evidence; the physical part of a critical behaviour comes only from the deterministic actions named in `critical_act_evidence`. `CommunicationScorer` converts the grades into the communication score (experimental formula in `content/scoring/rubric.json`) added to the process score.
3. **Retired and removed**: the hybrid-head classifier and both heads, the two-head agreement, the critical-mass cap and policies, critical cues, the assertion layer, the interpretation service and validation, the earlier semantic reader and verifier, per-act thresholds, `/classify` and `/interpret`, the classifier benchmark, recall, cap, NLI, rubric and safety-gate harnesses, their tests and the mutation catalogue built on them; the `classifier*` and `speech_interpretation` gate features. Result files, research pages, datasets and the frozen unsafe-speech sets stay as history.
4. **Evaluation**: three full runs on the 493-item development set and two controlled partial-credit sets, all labelled synthetic development evidence (`docs/research/gemma_evaluator_results.md`). Gate `speech_evaluation` defined and OFF.

## Why
The classifier path lost legitimate credit and was repaired by layer upon layer of vetoes. A model that reads the sentence in its scene made fewer semantic errors on the same items; the things that must stay deterministic (physical actions, state, windows, arithmetic) are kept in the engine.

## Status (22 October 2026)

**APPROVED and closed.** Held-out v1 was not approved; after one prompt refinement (`gemma-eval-5`) held-out v2 passed every pre-registered criterion except controlled partial-credit bands (25/32 against 0.80). The owner froze the architecture, prompt, scoring and thresholds and asked for the genuine contract defect to be fixed (DT-247: quoted evidence capped at 160 characters rejected valid evaluations of long natural speech; now up to 400 characters, still copied from the utterance, engine span cap 400) and for one fresh frozen 32-item partial-credit set: 27/32 = 84.4 percent in band (target 80, unchanged), 32/32 valid replies, 0 length rejections. Gate `speech_evaluation` ON from `reports/ai/speech-evaluation-acceptance.json`. Evidence is AI-written and AI-reviewed synthetic data, each set scored once against criteria fixed beforehand: not independent, not real audio. The real Meta Quest and ASR end-to-end validation is DC-10 (Sannan), not part of this step's acceptance. No push.
