# p1-ai-language-s11 — Interpretation service: candidates, status and deterministic validation

Milestone: **M10 P1 platform — backend, AI, voice input**

## What
`POST /interpret/organisations/{id}` with `{context (the s10 payload), utterance, utterance_id, clarifications_used}` returns `{decision, candidates[], evidence?, clarify?, menu[], reasons[]}`. Decisions: `OFFER_EVIDENCE` (a validated, non-critical, eligible act the engine may submit), `CLARIFY`, `ABSTAIN`, `MENU`. Nothing is credited here: the engine's `EvidenceStore` decides (ADR-0022).

1. **Candidates** (`server/interpret/candidates.py`): the retained gate of ADR-0020 (`EnsembleActClassifier`: hybrid head, TF-IDF agreement, critical-mass cap, critical cues, assertion check) gives its ranked acts; only acts in `context.eligible` are kept. More than one act may be kept when the head ranks two eligible acts close together and both pass every check (multi-intent; the rubric allows one sentence to do two things); otherwise the top one.
2. **Status** (`server/interpret/status.py`): actual, question, negated, intended, hypothetical, reported, other_actor, incomplete, ambiguous, from `server/classify/assertion.py` plus question and speaker checks. Deterministic.
3. **Validation** (`server/interpret/validate.py`), every rule only removes credit: act eligible now; not critical; status `actual`, or `question` for the acts that are questions (ASK_TOPIC, CLARIFY, CHECK_UNDERSTANDING, ESCALATE_SPEECH); a span of the utterance supports it; ANSWER_QUESTION only with `patient_question_pending`; CLOSED_LOOP only with `readback_pending`; behaviour not in `context.accepted`; the classifier's confidence above the act's threshold. Reason codes per refusal.
4. **Decision** (`server/interpret/decide.py`): one validated act → `OFFER_EVIDENCE`; two close eligible candidates, or an eligible act refused only for low confidence → `CLARIFY` (s12) while `clarifications_used` is under its limit; a critical topic → `MENU` pointing at the action the engine verifies; nothing eligible or anything refused for safety → `ABSTAIN` (no question: a refusal is not asked about).
5. Thin router `server/routers/interpret.py`, schemas `server/schemas/interpret.py`, audited like `/classify` (hash of the text, decision and reason codes), the AI rate limiter, behind the gate `speech_interpretation` (OFF: answers `MENU` with `FEATURE_OFF`).

## Why
Credit must depend on the state the trainee is in and on what the sentence actually asserts; both checks are rules, so they belong outside any model, and the fastest measured candidate generator is the one already retained (about 15 ms).

## How to implement
Folder `server/interpret/` (own responsibility, INSTRUCTIONS section 2); reuse `server/classify` unchanged; read the shared vectors of s10.

## Verification
`server/tests/test_interpret_*.py` and `tools/ci/test_interpretation_regression.py` (registered in `tools/ci/run_all.py`): out-of-window act refused; ANSWER_QUESTION without a pending question refused; CLOSED_LOOP without a read-back refused; the confusion pairs of ADR-0020 (CLARIFY/CHECK_UNDERSTANDING, ESCALATE_SPEECH/VOICE_ORDER, CLOSED_LOOP/ACKNOWLEDGE_FACT, EXPLAIN_ACTION/EXPLAIN_PROCEDURE, ANSWER_QUESTION/EXPLAIN_PROCEDURE) separated or clarified by context; negation, hypothetical, intention, reported, other speaker and incomplete never offered; critical content never offered; duplicate refused; a hostile classifier stub that names a critical or out-of-window act at confidence 1.0 is refused; gate off gives the menu.
