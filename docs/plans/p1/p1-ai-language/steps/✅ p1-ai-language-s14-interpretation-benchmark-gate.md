# p1-ai-language-s14 — Interpretation benchmark, end-to-end latency and the `speech_interpretation` gate

Milestone: **M10 P1 platform — backend, AI, voice input**

## What
A fair evaluation of the integrated path (s10 to s12) against the unchanged classifier route, and the gate that keeps speech-based evidence off until it passes (ADR-0022, decision 8).

1. **Context-conditioned adapter for the existing benchmark** (`tools/bench/interpretation_bench.py`): every row of `tests/action_bench_v1` is evaluated (a) with the context of a step inside its act's behaviour window, (b) with the context of a step outside every window of its act, and (c) for ANSWER_QUESTION and CLOSED_LOOP rows, with and without the pending question or read-back. Expected results follow from the approved mapping and the row's construction label (no model labels anything). Dev is used for any change; test and test2 are scored once. Synthetic and preliminary.
2. **Metrics**, against the unchanged gate on identical rows: per-class P/R/F1, macro-F1, confusion; legitimate recall and credited precision; critical, non-assertive and out-of-window false credits with denominators; coverage, abstention, clarification and legitimate-rejection rates; malformed and timeout counts.
3. **Latency, measured separately and end to end** (`tools/bench/interpretation_latency.py`): recogniser on the committed clips, interpretation route, validation, dialogue reply, with cold start and with a dialogue call competing for the model slot; p50/p95.
4. **Gate** `speech_interpretation` in `content/ai/thresholds.json`, read by `tools/eval/ai_gate.py`. Proposed thresholds, **PENDING the owner's approval** (DT-229): on the independent set (EX-01) critical false credit 0; non-assertive false credit at most 1 percent; out-of-window false credit 0; credited precision at least 0.95; legitimate recall at least 0.50 and not below the classifier route's; interpretation p95 at most 300 ms; voice round trip p95 at most 4 s (C31); every failure case of s12 tested. Synthetic results are recorded for information only and can never switch the gate on.

## Why
The previous experiments measured components on sentences without state; the decision to ship must rest on the integrated path, with context, on data nobody on the build side wrote.

## Status (19 October 2026)
Built and measured (19 Oct 2026), synthetic and preliminary. The interpretation path offers exactly what the classifier route offers after the engine's window rule (test 66 of 66 right, test2 69 of 69; legitimate recall 66 of 140 and 69 of 140, unchanged) and refuses what the route credits without state: 0 critical, 0 non-assertive and 0 out-of-window offers. One interpretation: median 16 ms, p95 30 ms (24.5 ms p95 while the 4B model generates). The gate stays OFF and SKIPPED: it reads the independent file, which does not exist (EX-01); the thresholds are the engineer's proposal (DT-229).

## Dependencies

## Common issues
