# p1-ai-language-s12 — Clarification, abstention, UI fallback and failure handling

Milestone: **M10 P1 platform — backend, AI, voice input**

## What
What the trainee experiences when the interpretation is not sure, and what happens when a service fails (ADR-0022, decision 5):

* **CLARIFY:** one short question naming the two candidates in plain words ("Did you mean to explain the operation, or to answer her question?"), built from approved act labels (`content/assessment/taxonomy.json`), never from the model. Limit: one clarification per utterance (`clarifications_used` < 1); the answer is a new utterance interpreted again with the same context.
* **MENU:** the existing menu actions of the eligible objectives' behaviours (`mapping.json` `actions`), so the trainee can select what they meant; for a critical topic the menu points at the verified action (wristband, consent form, checklist).
* **ABSTAIN:** nothing is credited and nothing is asked; the conversation continues.
* **Failures:** timeout (the live budget of `unity_ai_hooks.md`), malformed response, model or embedder unavailable, cancelled request (the trainee spoke again or left the step), gate off: `MENU`, no credit, logged with a reason code. No retry loop: at most one retry of a transport error inside the timeout, never a recursive call.

Unity half: `unity/Assets/Scripts/Voice/SpeechInterpretation.cs` (builds the request from the engine's `InterpretationContext`, calls the route with a timeout and cancellation, turns `OFFER_EVIDENCE` into a `speech_evidence` action applied to the session, shows the clarification or the menu); EditMode tests with a fake transport. Scene wiring and the headset run are Sannan's (DC-08, DC-09).

## Why
A forced guess is a wrong credit or a wrong refusal; a clear question or the menu keeps the trainee moving and keeps the score honest.

## How to implement
`server/interpret/decide.py` (limits, texts from the taxonomy), `server/interpret/fallback.py` (menu from the mapping), the Unity script and its EditMode tests, the `speech_interpretation` entries of `docs/architecture/unity_ai_hooks.md`.

## Verification
Tests: the clarification limit is enforced (a second CLARIFY becomes MENU); the clarification text contains no model output; timeout, malformed JSON, unavailable embedder, cancelled request and gate off each give MENU with their reason code and no evidence; no retry beyond one; Unity EditMode: an `OFFER_EVIDENCE` reply is applied once, a duplicate reply is not applied twice, a late reply after cancellation is dropped.

## Files changed
`server/interpret/decide.py` (the clarification limit, the question built from `content/ai/interpret_phrases.json`, the menu from `mapping.json`; there is no separate `fallback.py`), `server/routers/interpret.py` (gate off, busy slot, any error: the menu), `unity/Assets/Scripts/Voice/SpeechInterpretation.cs` (+ `.meta`), `unity/Assets/Scripts/Bridge/SimulationHost.cs` (`Content`), `unity/Assets/Tests/EditMode/SpeechInterpretationTests.cs`, `docs/architecture/unity_ai_hooks.md` (hook 13), `tools/ci/test_interpretation_regression.py`, `server/tests/test_interpret_api.py`.

## Dependencies
p1-ai-language-s11.

## Common issues
A clarification answered with "yes": the answer is a new utterance and is interpreted with `clarifications_used` 1, so it can lead to an offer or the menu but never to a second question; a bare "yes" names no act and gets the menu. The menu tap, not the spoken reply, is the reliable answer to the question.
